What was the distribution? On 3 November 2021, International Business Machines Corporation (“IBM” or “we”) distributed 80.1% of its interest in Kyndryl Holdings, Inc. (“Kyndryl”) to holders of its common stock. Holders of IBM common stock received one share of Kyndryl common stock for every five shares of IBM common stock held as of the close of business on the record date, 25 October 2021. When was the distribution? The distribution was effective as of 5:00 PM, Eastern time, on 3 November 2021. Did the number of IBM shares I own change as a result of the distribution? The transaction had no impact on the number of shares of IBM common stock that you own. Unless you sold or otherwise disposed of your IBM shares, your balance before and after the transaction remained the same.
