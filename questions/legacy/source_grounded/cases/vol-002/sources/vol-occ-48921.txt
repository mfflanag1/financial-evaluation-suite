Date: June 29, 2021
Subject: General Electric Company - Reverse Split
Option Symbols: GE/1GE/2GE
New Symbols: GE1/1GE1/2GE1
Date: 8/2/21
General Electric Company (GE) has announced a 1-for-8 reverse stock split. As a result of the reverse
stock split, each GE Common Share will be converted into the right to receive 0.125 (New) General
Electric Company Common Shares. The reverse stock split will become effective before the market open
on August 2, 2021. Cash will be paid in lieu of fractional GE shares.
