Background
3M Company (MMM) has announced a distribution of (New) Solventum Corporation (SOLV) Common
Shares. The distribution ratio is 0.25 of a SOLV share for each MMM share held. The record date is March
18, 2024; the payable date is April 1, 2024. The NYSE has set April 1, 2024, as the ex-distribution date for
this distribution.
Solventum Corporation Common Shares began trading on a when issued basis on March 26, 2024 on the
NYSE under the trading symbol “SOLV WI”.
